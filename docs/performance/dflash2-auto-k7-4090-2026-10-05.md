# RTX 4090 DFlash2 Auto K7-baseline follow-up — 2026-10-05

## Decision

Keep standalone **Fixed K7** as the production default. Schema-3 Auto Selected is now a safe
opt-in: its K7 default removes the old uncovered-cell K0 regression, and its concurrent result is
close to Fixed K7. It still does not qualify as the default because no held-out condition proves at
least 2% improvement over the best fixed policy in both AB/BA orientations.

The distinction is deliberate. This campaign shows that Auto Selected has recovered Fixed-K7-like
performance; it does not show incremental value sufficient to justify the calibrated profile,
startup-K15 resource contract and routing complexity.

## Result

The campaign used two RTX 4090 GPUs, the explicit
`Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B-ninfer-v3.ninfer` artifact, INT8 KV, CUDA Graphs, greedy zero-penalty
sampling, a 512-token output budget, C1/C2/C4/C8, two AB/BA pairs and two repeats. The complete
local result is `profiles/bench/pr16-auto-k7-2026-10-05/summary.json`.

Geometric-mean speedup over backend None:

| Scope | Fixed K7 | Auto Full | Auto Selected |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Single, cold | 2.094× | 1.995× | 2.103× |
| Single, warm | 2.731× | 2.588× | 2.771× |
| Concurrent, cold | 1.433× | 1.287× | 1.419× |
| Concurrent, warm | 1.740× | 1.492× | 1.728× |

The old 2026-10-04 Auto Selected concurrent results were 1.021× cold and 1.003× warm. The new
1.419×/1.728× results confirm that schema 3 fixes the dominant K0-fallback regression and brings
Auto Selected close to Fixed K7's 1.433×/1.740×. Closeness is not an Auto qualification: near ties
resolve to the simpler fixed policy.

## Why Auto Selected is not the default

Auto must beat the best fixed arm in every retained pair, reach at least 2% median improvement and
keep paired request-p95 regression within 5%. Auto Selected was correct in the reused correctness
scope and faster than None, but `qualified_vs_best_fixed` is false for every held-out condition.

The E8 full and selected profiles contain no override: they use `default_action: 7` for all cells.
At C2/C4/C8, Auto therefore reproduces K7's action rather than discovering a better policy, while
measured throughput is generally 0–2.8% below Fixed K7. The E1 profiles contain one measured K11
override for active batch 1 and frontier at most 8192. Its held-out benefit is not stable enough to
clear the qualification gate; even the favorable warm medium-context pair has approximately 0.76%
and 2.33% gains, whose median is below 2%.

Fixed K7 consequently provides the same practical performance class without a calibration file,
profile-identity matching, startup K15 or route-selection policy. Auto Selected remains useful for
continued calibration work, but it should replace Fixed K7 only after held-out overrides demonstrate
repeatable incremental value.

## Evidence boundary

This follow-up ran the performance campaign only. It reused the prior completed 18-case correctness
and five-check ownership evidence and did not rerun the native correctness suite, CPU/C++/Python
regressions or CI. The performance summary reports `complete: true` and `correct: true`, with no
failure artifact; `correct` includes that explicitly reused correctness evidence.
